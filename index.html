联系开发者

如果遇到问题或有功能建议，请发送邮件。反馈问题时，可以附上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、App 版本和操作步骤。请先遮盖截图中的出生日期、私人日子和照片。

添加小组件

长按 iPhone 主屏幕空白处，打开添加小组件界面，搜索「度量时间」，然后选取时间进度或重要日子组件。

管理重要日子

点首页的「事」查看重要日子。进入日子详情后，从照片按钮移除配图，或从更多操作菜单删除整条日子记录。

Contact the developer

Email us with questions, issues, or suggestions. When reporting an issue, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce it if you can. Please cover birth dates, personal events, and photos in any screenshots you send.

Add a widget

Touch and hold an empty area on your iPhone Home Screen, open the widget picker, search for MeasureTime or 度量时间, and choose a time progress or important day widget.

Manage important days

Tap 事 on the home screen to see your important days. Open a day to remove its picture from the photo menu or delete the day from the more actions menu.
