App 如何处理数据

度量时间无需账号，也不会向开发者发送 App 使用数据。App 不包含广告、分析或跟踪。显示设置、你填写的出生日期与预期寿命、重要日子记录和你选作配图的照片副本，都保存在设备上，用于计算和显示时间进度、展示日子和提供小组件。

照片和分享

添加配图时，App 使用 iOS 的照片选择器，只读取你选中的照片。你可以通过 iOS 分享面板把生成的图片发送到你选择的应用或联系人。你选择保存图片时，App 可请求仅添加照片的权限，把图片写入系统照片图库。你可在 iOS 设置中管理这一权限。分享后的图片由你选择的接收方或服务处理。

删除和备份

你可以在 App 内移除配图或删除重要日子。使用 iOS 的「删除 App」可删除本机 App 及其本地数据。已经保存到照片图库或分享出去的图片需在对应位置另行删除。如果你启用了 iCloud 备份、设备备份或 iCloud 照片，系统可能会备份 App 数据或同步你保存的照片。你可以通过 Apple 提供的设置管理相关备份和同步。

支持网站和邮件

支持和隐私页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问页面时，GitHub 会为安全目的记录并保存访问者的 IP 地址。本网站不添加分析或跟踪脚本。你发送支持邮件时，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址及邮件内容，并保留处理和跟进请求所需的信息。你可以用下方邮箱咨询隐私问题或请求删除支持邮件中的个人信息。

How the app handles data

MeasureTime requires no account and sends no app usage data to the developer. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking. Display preferences, any birth date and expected lifespan you enter, important day records, and copies of photos you choose are stored on your device to calculate and display progress, show important days, and provide widgets.

Photos and sharing

The app uses the iOS photo picker and reads only the photo you select to illustrate a day. The iOS share sheet lets you send a generated image to an app or recipient of your choice. If you choose to save an image, the app may request add-only permission to write it to your Photos library. You can manage that permission in iOS Settings. The recipient or service you choose handles images you share.

Deletion and backups

You can remove a picture or delete an important day in the app. Using Delete App in iOS removes the app and its local data from your device. Delete copies saved to Photos or shared elsewhere separately at their destinations. If you enable iCloud Backup, device backups, or iCloud Photos, the system may back up app data or sync photos you save. You can manage those backups and sync settings through Apple.

Support website and email

GitHub Pages hosts these support and privacy pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security when they visit. This site adds no analytics or tracking scripts. If you email support, the developer receives your email address and message, and retains the information needed to handle and follow up on your request.
